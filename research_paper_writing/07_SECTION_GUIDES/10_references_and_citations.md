## What this section must accomplish

Citations are load-bearing claims about the literature: every one asserts that a source says
what you say it says. That is why ARES treats an unresolvable DOI as a critical flaw, and why
this library's tools section centers on reference management. Style (APA, IEEE, Vancouver,
Chicago) is venue-determined and mechanical — Zotero handles it; correctness of *content* (right
paper, right claim, right year) is scholarly judgment and must be verified by hand or by
`09_citation_mastery/`.

## Working rules

**The citation hygiene protocol**
1. Manage in Zotero from day one — never retype a reference
2. Cite from the source: read what you cite (SciSpace comprehension helps; the paper itself is final)
3. Check format against the venue template's example (IEEE numeric vs. author-date are not interchangeable)
4. Run `09_citation_mastery/multi_format_citations.md` for the venue's exact style
5. Audit for: orphan claims (no citation), citation-claim mismatch, duplicates, and stale
   versions of datasets/software (cite the *version you used*)

**Ethics edge**: self-plagiarism needs citation too; quote or cite your own prior text — MIT
21W.794's source-work module covers the boundary.

## Session protocol

1. **Watch** the paired Ref-n-Write video (5–10 minutes).
2. **Read** this guide's working rules once.
3. **Draft or revise** your section applying one rule at a time — not all at once.
4. **Run** `skills/09_citation_mastery/bibliography_builder.md` on the result.
5. **Score** with `evaluators/CLARITY_ANALYZER.md` (target ≥80) and, where the section carries
   empirical claims, `evaluators/RIGOR_RUBRIC.md`.
