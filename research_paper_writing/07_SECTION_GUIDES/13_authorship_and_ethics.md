## What this section must accomplish

Authorship is an ethical claim about contribution, not a courtesy or an ordering puzzle: the
standard (ICMJE and most venues) is substantial intellectual contribution plus accountability
for the work. Getting it wrong — gift authorship, ghost authorship, retaliation ordering — is
an integrity violation that can surface years later. Wiley's course devotes modules to the
author list and acknowledgements precisely because these are decisions, not afterthoughts.

## Working rules

**The authorship decision set**
1. **Who qualifies**: substantial contribution to conception/design/execution/analysis *and*
   drafting or critically revising *and* final approval *and* accountability (ICMJE's four
   criteria, adopted widely beyond medicine)
2. **CRediT roles**: state contributions explicitly (14 standard roles) where the venue allows
   — it defuses ordering disputes
3. **Order**: field conventions differ (alphabetical in some areas, contribution-weighted in
   others); agree *before* drafting, in writing
4. **Who doesn't qualify but helped**: acknowledged, with permission, and funding disclosed
5. **Compliance sweep**: IRB/ethics numbers, consent, data-availability statement, conflicts
   of interest, funding sources, pre-registration IDs — `08_ethics_compliance/reproducibility_audit.md`
   runs the full checklist

ACS Author University's *Deciding Authorship Order* episode covers the negotiation tactfully.
