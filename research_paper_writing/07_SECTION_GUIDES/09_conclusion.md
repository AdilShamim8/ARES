## What this section must accomplish

The conclusion closes the loop: answer the RQ in one or two sentences, restate the
contribution in its most compact form, and open exactly one next question. It is short (a
paragraph in most journals; some venues merge it into the Discussion) — which is why it is
often written carelessly. A weak conclusion spends its length re-summarizing the paper; a
strong one spends it on what the reader should now believe and do.

## Working rules

**The three-sentence conclusion**
1. **Answer**: "We showed that X, by [method in six words], achieving [headline result]."
2. **Significance**: what this changes — for the sub-question, the field, or practice
3. **Horizon**: the next question this opens (which conveniently seeds your next paper —
   `10_impact_amplifier/follow_up_research_engine.md` runs exactly this move)

**Do not**: introduce new results, new citations, or new claims in the conclusion. If a new
idea belongs, it belonged in the Discussion.
