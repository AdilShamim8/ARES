## What this section must accomplish

The methods section is the paper's reproducibility contract: a competent stranger in your
field should be able to rerun the study from the text alone. That standard — not "sound
like a scientist" — decides what stays and what goes. Past tense, passive or active per venue
norm, and complete: materials with sources and versions, procedures in order, parameters with
values, statistical tests with assumptions stated.

## Working rules

**The completeness audit**
- Materials: what, how much, from where, which version/lot/vendor — and availability statement
- Procedure: ordered steps; randomized/blinded conditions stated for studies with them
- Parameters: every number with units and the reason for its value (or a sensitivity note)
- Statistics: test, effect size measure, significance threshold, software + version
- Ethics: IRB/ethics approval number, consent, pre-registration ID where applicable

**Length signal**: methods are the section reviewers most often call "under-specified" — when
in doubt, add the detail; move exhaustive material to supplementaries, not to oblivion.
MIT's 21W.794 source-work materials apply directly (paraphrase protocols, not copy-paste from
prior methods sections — or cite your own past text).

## Session protocol

1. **Watch** the paired Ref-n-Write video (5–10 minutes).
2. **Read** this guide's working rules once.
3. **Draft or revise** your section applying one rule at a time — not all at once.
4. **Run** `skills/05_writing_excellence/methods_precision.md` on the result.
5. **Score** with `evaluators/CLARITY_ANALYZER.md` (target ≥80) and, where the section carries
   empirical claims, `evaluators/RIGOR_RUBRIC.md`.
