## What this section must accomplish

The discussion is where findings become meaning: interpret against the RQ, compare with the
literature the review established, separate what the data proves from what it suggests, and
acknowledge limits honestly. The strongest discussions feel like a conversation with the
field's best critics — because that is exactly the audience. The Ref-n-Write video spends its
longest runtime here (9:45), which is the right instinct: more papers die in the Discussion
than anywhere but the Introduction.

## Working rules

**The discussion skeleton**
1. **Restate the finding** (one sentence, no new numbers)
2. **Interpret**: what it means — mechanism, why the result came out this way
3. **Contextualize**: agreement/conflict with prior work (cite the review's anchors), and why the difference
4. **Limitations**: honest, specific, and each paired with its effect on the conclusion
5. **Implications**: what changes for theory/practice
6. **Future work**: one or two concrete next studies, not a wish list

**The overclaim test** (what `06_quality_fortress/logic_validator.md` runs): strike every
sentence the data cannot carry — "proves", "demonstrates conclusively", generalizations beyond
the sample. Hedge precisely: "suggests within this cohort" beats vague hedging.

## Session protocol

1. **Watch** the paired Ref-n-Write video (5–10 minutes).
2. **Read** this guide's working rules once.
3. **Draft or revise** your section applying one rule at a time — not all at once.
4. **Run** `skills/05_writing_excellence/discussion_depth.md` on the result.
5. **Score** with `evaluators/CLARITY_ANALYZER.md` (target ≥80) and, where the section carries
   empirical claims, `evaluators/RIGOR_RUBRIC.md`.
