# The Scientist's Guide to Writing — Stephen B. Heard

| | |
|:---|:---|
| **Author** | Stephen B. Heard (University of New Brunswick — ecology) |
| **Publisher** | Princeton University Press — 1st ed. 2016; **2nd ed. 2022** (Princeton announced a 3rd edition as well) |
| **Cost** | Paid book |

## What it covers

How to write *more easily and effectively throughout a scientific career*: the psychology of
writing habits, revision as a discipline, dealing with writer's block, writing with
co-authors, and advice for grad students and advisors — wrapping craft in a humane,
research-based treatment of the writing life.

## Why it is recommended here

The best book on the *process* layer (vs. product layer): productivity, habits, and the
emotional reality of writing. Pairs perfectly with Dr Amina Yonis' channel for the practice
side, and its "write regularly, revise ruthlessly" doctrine is what ARES's staged workflows
mechanize.
