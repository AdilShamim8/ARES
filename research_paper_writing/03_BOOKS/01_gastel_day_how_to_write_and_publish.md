## What it covers

The classic, now in its ninth edition: the whole arc — what a scientific paper is, IMRAD section
by section, how to write each one well, ethics and authorship, choosing a journal, the
submission and review process, conferences, posters, and the modern ecosystem (preprints, open
access). Bloomsbury's listing summarizes it as "thoroughly updated throughout… classic,
practical text on how to write and publish a scientific paper" that postgraduates and working
scientists keep on the shelf.

## Why it is recommended here

This is *the* reference textbook of the field — the one book that has survived nine editions and
decades of use across every discipline. If you buy one book from this library, buy this one: it
answers "how does the whole system work" from a pair of authors who ran it (Day as editor of
*ASTM Journal of Testing and Evaluation*; Gastel as professor of science communication and
veterinary integrative biosciences at Texas A&M).
