## What it covers

Genre-based academic writing training: writing strategies, summary and critique, data
commentary, argument, and — famously — the **CARS model** (Create a Research Space) for research
paper introductions, which Swales originated. Task-and-skill organization with extensive
exercises.

## Why it is recommended here

The origin point of "moves" analysis: every modern introduction-writing guide (including the
Ref-n-Write video and ARES's `introduction_craft.md`) descends from Swales' work. For graduate
writers who want the research-tested theory of how academic texts actually persuade, this is
the source text.
