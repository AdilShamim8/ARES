## What it covers

The ESL-first treatment of paper writing: planning and preparation, which section to write in
what order, clarity and concision for non-native speakers, ambiguity traps, and the
genre-specific English of each manuscript section. Earlier editions note coverage of reviewer
complaints ("what referees criticize and why"). Springer records strong citation impact for the
work (its link page shows hundreds of citing works across editions).

## Why it is recommended here

The world's standard book for the majority of authors who write in English as a second
language. Where Schimel tells stories and Alley polishes mechanics, Wallwork attacks the
specific failure modes of ESL academic prose — the exact prose that `evaluators/CLARITY_ANALYZER.md`
tends to flag most.
