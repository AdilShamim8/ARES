## What it covers

Science as **story**: how to build narrative structure into papers and proposals — openings,
challenge/action/resolution arcs, paragraph-level story flow, and how strong storytelling
raises citation and funding odds. OUP's own page positions it as "a much-needed guide to
succeeding in modern science… insights and strategies [that] will equip science students,
scientists, and professionals."

## Why it is recommended here

The community's favorite "second book" — where Gastel & Day teach the *system*, Schimel teaches
the *voice*. Reviewers and blogs consistently recommend it for researchers whose papers are
technically correct but read flat. ARES's clarity engine rewards exactly the active-verb,
concrete-subject prose Schimel coaches.
