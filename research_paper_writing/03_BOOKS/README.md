# 03 · Books — the canonical bookshelf

Eight books, one per tradition: system (Gastel & Day), story (Schimel), mechanics (Alley),
ESL-English (Wallwork, Glasman-Deal), process (Heard), theory (Williams & Bizup, Swales & Feak).

| # | Book | Author | Publisher / Edition | Best for |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 01 | How to Write and Publish a Scientific Paper | Gastel & Day | Cambridge UP, 9th ed. (2022) | The whole system |
| 02 | Writing Science | Schimel | Oxford UP (2012) | Story craft |
| 03 | The Craft of Scientific Writing | Alley | Springer | Sentence mechanics |
| 04 | English for Writing Research Papers | Wallwork | Springer, 4th ed. | Non-native English |
| 05 | Science Research Writing | Glasman-Deal | World Scientific | Section anatomy |
| 06 | The Scientist's Guide to Writing | Heard | Princeton UP, 2nd ed. | Process & habits |
| 07 | Style: Lessons in Clarity and Grace | Williams & Bizup | Pearson, 11th ed. | Theory of clarity |
| 08 | Academic Writing for Graduate Students | Swales & Feak | U. Michigan Press, 3rd ed. | Genre & moves |

Buy in this order: **01 → 02 or 04 → 06**. Everything else is library-loan or specialty.
*All bibliographic facts verified 5 November 2026.*
