## What it covers

The theory of clear prose: subjects and actions, characters and topics, emphasis positions,
cohesion and coherence — the machinery every modern writing course (including Stanford's)
quietly teaches from. Wikipedia's entry documents its multi-decade, multi-edition status as the
standard university style text.

## Why it is recommended here

If you want to know *why* Sainani's "verbs" unit works, or why the phrasebank's topic-sentence
patterns read clearly, it is in this book. Recommended for the researcher who wants one level
deeper than any course gives — the linguistics of clarity.
