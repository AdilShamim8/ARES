## What it covers

A **structure-then-language** walk through each section of a science paper — for each: the
canonical move structure, the grammar that performs those moves, and the vocabulary that fills
them. Edinburgh's library record describes the 2021 edition (World Scientific, New Jersey) as
covering writing for native and non-native speakers alike. Cited by ~374 works per World
Scientific's own count.

## Why it is recommended here

The tightest "section anatomy" book in print: each chapter dissects one manuscript section into
its rhetorical moves and gives the sentence-level English for each move. If Wallwork is the
broad treatment, Glasman-Deal is the scalpel — and her Imperial College affiliation means the
book teaches the exact register of UK/European journals.
