| **Certificate** | Yes — including the **Certified Peer Reviewer Course** |

## What it covers

A publisher-grade e-learning platform spanning the whole research journey. Its flagship for
authors is the **Certified Peer Reviewer Course** — an on-demand, free course whose module list
includes *What is peer review?, Models of peer review, The typical peer review process, How to
get involved*, plus ethics and writing an effective review report, ending in an assessment.
Enrollment is free with registration.

## Why it is recommended here

Two reasons. First, it is one of the few places you can be *certified* in peer review at zero
cost, which doubles as writing education (knowing how papers are judged makes you a better
author). Second, its "Fundamentals of manuscript preparation" and "Writing skills" tracks are
written by a major publisher's editorial staff — the exact audience your submission faces.
