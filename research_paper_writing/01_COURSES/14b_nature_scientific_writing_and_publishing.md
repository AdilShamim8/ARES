## What it covers

The umbrella line comprising *Writing a Research Paper*, *Publishing a Research Paper*, and peer-
review/related modules — taught by 36 working Nature Portfolio editors in ~10-minute lessons
across ≈21 hours. Harvard's career-navigator page describes working through the three courses in
order or dipping into modules.

## Why it is recommended here

The deepest editor-taught video library in this catalog: 21 hours of editors explaining what
they look for. If your institution subscribes (many do — check your library before paying), it
is the best single subscription for a lab or department to share.

## How to use it with ARES

- Work through in order while running `workflows/FULL_PAPER_WORKFLOW.md` — one course phase per workflow phase
- Sample lesson first (free) to see whether the format suits you
