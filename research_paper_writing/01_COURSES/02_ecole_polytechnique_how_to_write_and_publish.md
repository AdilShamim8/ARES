## What it covers (4 modules, project-centered)

You will: outline a complete scientific paper, choose an appropriate journal for the finished
paper, and build a checklist to judge whether your paper is ready to submit. The course page
positions it for students with prior research experience who want to publish their own work.

## Why it is recommended here

It is the strongest *project-centered* option: instead of quizzes you finish holding an outline,
a target journal, and a submission-readiness checklist. That artifact-first design maps directly
onto ARES's workflow style, and the Coursera page notes each module runs 3–4 hours — genuinely
completable in a working week.

## How to use it with ARES

- The paper outline you produce → feed to `skills/01_ideation/gap_analysis_prompt.md`
- The journal choice → validate with `skills/07_journal_strategy/venue_matcher.md`
- The submission checklist → cross-check against `evaluators/` go/no-go gates (Novelty ≥70, Rigor ≥75, Clarity ≥80, Impact ≥75)
