## What it covers

Per ACS's own announcement: peer-review basics, publishing ethics, and practical guidance on how
to write a high-quality review report — with a final assessment and certificate. Developed by
ACS staff and journal editors.

## Why it is recommended here

Chemistry's standard bearer for reviewer training, from the society that publishes *JACS* and
*ACS Nano*. Like Elsevier's Certified Peer Reviewer Course, it converts you into a better author
by teaching you to read as a reviewer; unlike it, this one is a compact six modules you can
finish in a weekend. Also the natural prerequisite for accepting your first ACS review invite —
which itself is the best writing education there is.

## How to use it with ARES

- Finish a module, then run `skills/06_quality_fortress/expert_paper_reviewer.md` on a real (published) paper to practice
- Use its ethics module alongside `skills/08_ethics_compliance/`
