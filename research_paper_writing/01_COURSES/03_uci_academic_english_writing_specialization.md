# Academic English: Writing Specialization — UC Irvine

| | |
|:---|:---|
| **Provider** | University of California, Irvine (Division of Continuing Education) |
| **Platform** | Coursera — <https://www.coursera.org/specializations/academic-english> |
| **Cost** | Free to audit each course; paid certificate / specialization option |
| **Level / effort** | Beginner · 5-course series · each course ≈4 weeks |
| **Format** | Graded quizzes, peer-reviewed essays, a capstone research paper |

## What it covers (5 courses)

1. **Grammar and Punctuation** — sentence types, commas, parallel structure
2. **Getting Started with Essay Writing** — thesis statements, body paragraphs, introductions/conclusions
3. **Advanced Writing** — elevated academic tone, synthesis, argument
4. **Introduction to Research for Essay Writing** — credible sources, annotated bibliography, research-paper planning
5. **Project: Writing a Research Paper** (capstone) — 6 modules: Getting Started → Research and Annotated Bibliography → First Draft and Introduction Paragraph → The Rough Draft → Revise and … final polish

## Why it is recommended here

The only major MOOC specialization whose *capstone deliverable is a complete research paper*,
built module by module from annotated bibliography to final revision. Coursera's own catalog
ranks it #1 in its Academic Writing category, and it is the safest on-ramp for researchers whose
English needs scaffolding before scientific-specific courses. Course 4's source-credibility and
citation training is directly transferable to literature work.
