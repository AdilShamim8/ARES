## What it covers

Beyond writing, the Researcher Academy organizes tracks for **research preparation, funding,
research data management, writing for research, fundamentals of manuscript preparation, writing
skills, and the publication process**. This profile collects the non-writing tracks that still
shape a paper's fate: data-management plans increasingly required by journals and funders, and
funding applications whose narrative reuses your paper's argument structure.

## Why it is recommended here

A paper is downstream of a well-managed project: shared data, a DMP, and a funded plan. These
free modules cover the compliance layer most writing courses ignore, and they come from a
publisher that enforces those requirements at submission.
