## What it covers

Everything needed to publish a book with a scholarly publisher: proposal writing, finding the
right editor/series, the review process, contracts, and production — the monograph path that
theses and review-of-field projects sometimes follow.

## Why it is recommended here

Research-paper libraries stop at articles; many researchers eventually need the *book* path
(thesis-to-monograph is the classic route in humanities and in CS/LNCS). This is the only
publisher-taught free mini-course on that route, from the house that runs LNCS and Palgrave.
