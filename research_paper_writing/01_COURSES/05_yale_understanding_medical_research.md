## What it covers

The course trains *critical consumption* of medical research: study designs, confounding,
relative vs. absolute risk, p-values and confidence intervals, and how to tell strong evidence
from weak. ModernMedEd's survey of medical-writing courses lists it among the top free options,
auditable on Coursera.

## Why it is recommended here

Before you can *write* convincing clinical evidence you must be able to *read* it — this is the
best-rated general course for that literacy. It pairs with the CDC/TEPHINET writing course for
researchers in health fields: Yale teaches what the evidence means; CDC/TEPHINET teaches how to
write it up.
