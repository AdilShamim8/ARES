## What it covers

Per the course page: discovering whether research is right for you, building confidence to
pursue it, and navigating the clinical-research landscape — the orientation course for medical
professionals considering research, rather than a drafting course.

## Why it is recommended here

For clinicians (the largest group of first-time authors who arrive without research training),
the bottleneck is *orientation* — study design, regulation, and where a paper fits in a research
career. Imperial's course covers that upstream layer, and Imperial's own student pages confirm
the free-audit route on edX.
