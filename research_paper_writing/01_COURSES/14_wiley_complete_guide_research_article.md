## Why it is recommended here

The most *complete section-by-section free course* in this catalog: every manuscript component
gets its own module, including the ones most courses skip (keywords, acknowledgements, cover
letter, author list). Community reviews (widely shared by researchers on LinkedIn and Facebook)
confirm it is free with a certificate. It maps one-to-one onto this library's own section guides
(`../07_SECTION_GUIDES/`).

## How to use it with ARES

- Do one module, then run the matching ARES section prompt (e.g., Abstract module → `skills/05_writing_excellence/abstract_mastery.md`)
- The final exam doubles as a pre-submission checklist — cross-check with `evaluators/` gates
