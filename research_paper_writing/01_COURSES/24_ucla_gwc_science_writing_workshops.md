## What it covers

Per the center's pages: practical tips for writing a scientific paper — planning, preparing, and
organizing science-specific writing including term papers, manuscripts, and abstracts — plus
recorded workshops on dissertation proposals, journal articles, and article-based dissertations
for the sciences and engineering.

## Why it is recommended here

University writing centers are the most under-used free resource in research writing, and
UCLA's publishes its recorded science-writing workshops openly. A recorded workshop is the right
size when you want one concrete topic (e.g., "organize a journal article") in under an hour
without enrolling in anything.
