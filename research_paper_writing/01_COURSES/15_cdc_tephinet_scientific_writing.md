# CDC/TEPHINET Scientific Writing Course

| | |
|:---|:---|
| **Provider** | U.S. CDC and TEPHINET (Task Force for Global Health), developed with Wisewire |
| **Platform** | <https://www.tephinet.org/tephinet-learning-center/tephinet-library/online-scientific-communication-courses-writing> |
| **Cost** | **Free** |
| **Format** | Self-guided e-learning · 8 interactive modules · complete at your own pace |
| **Audience** | First-time authors; public-health and field-epidemiology researchers |

## What it covers

Per CDC's archived course announcement and TEPHINET's listing: the process of writing and
submitting a scientific research manuscript, module by module — designed so learners can start
at any module and work at their own pace. The 2026 comparison at Manusights characterizes it as
"a procedural approach that walks through each IMRAD section in order — suits anyone drafting a
first paper who wants a checklist more than a writing philosophy."

## Why it is recommended here

The best free *first-paper* course with a public-health frame: genuinely interactive (not just
videos), built by CDC experts, and hosted on a training network that reaches researchers in the
countries that need free training most. If your first paper is a field-epidemiology or clinical
study, start here rather than with a general MOOC.
