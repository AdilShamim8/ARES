## What it covers

The full authoring journey in course form: manuscript structure, writing each section, journal
selection, submission mechanics, and peer response. A representative course, *How to Submit a
Journal Article and Get it Published*, covers authorship criteria, article types, and how
manuscript-submission systems work at popular journals.

## Why it is recommended here

The strongest catalog on *submission mechanics* — the systems, screens, and checklists of
Editorial Manager and friends — which no university MOOC teaches. Its "steps" structure lets you
jump straight to the submission step that is blocking you.
