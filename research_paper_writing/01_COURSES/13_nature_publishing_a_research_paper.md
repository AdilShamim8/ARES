## What it covers

Per the course page: how to write an engaging cover letter, make reviewer suggestions, understand
and fulfil open-science requirements, and register for an ORCID — a comprehensive, editor's-eye
overview of navigating from finished manuscript to accepted paper.

## Why it is recommended here

The strongest "last mile" course: cover letters, reviewer suggestions, open-science compliance,
ORCID — the operational details that decide whether a good manuscript survives editorial triage.
ARES's `skills/07_journal_strategy/cover_letter_engine.md` encodes the same moves; this course
teaches the reasoning behind them from the editor's chair.
