## What it covers

Field-specific writing & publishing training for small groups, including **one-to-one abstract
review** — a Nature editor reads your abstract and gives individual feedback. The workshops page
describes the full portfolio (on-demand courses, workshops, webinars, field-specific training).

## Why it is recommended here

The only entry in the courses folder where a *working journal editor reads your actual abstract*.
For a paper aimed at a Nature Portfolio journal, one round of that feedback is worth more than
any number of generic courses. Check the page for the current calendar and whether your
institution holds a Masterclasses subscription.

## How to use it with ARES

- Submit your abstract for review only *after* it passes `skills/05_writing_excellence/abstract_mastery.md` and the Clarity ≥80 gate — spend the editor's attention on a polished draft
