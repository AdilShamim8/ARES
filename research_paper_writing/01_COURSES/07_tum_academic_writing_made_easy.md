| **Format** | Structured MOOC with writing assignments |

## What it covers

A foundations course in academic writing craft — organizing ideas, paragraph logic, academic
register — aimed at university writers who want a systematic, exercise-driven path rather than
loose tips. edX course pages for TUMx offerings follow the audit-free model, and learner reviews
praise its structured pacing.

## Why it is recommended here

The strongest *European technical-university* writing MOOC still listed on edX. For engineers and
computer scientists who want writing taught with the same systematic framing as their own
discipline, TUM is a natural home, and it complements the MIT OCW workshops (which are materials,
not guided courses).
