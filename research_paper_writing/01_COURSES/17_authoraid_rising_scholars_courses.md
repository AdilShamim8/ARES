## What it covers

Per INASP/AuthorAID announcements: literature review, research and publication ethics, targeting
the right journals, writing the sections of a paper, dealing with peer review. The October 2025
announcement describes *Getting Started with Writing and Publishing Your Research* as a 6-week
course building research writing and publishing capability step by step.

## Why it is recommended here

The world's most important *free, human-mentored* writing program for researchers in low- and
middle-income countries: cohort courses with real moderators, peer review between participants,
and a route into AuthorAID's mentoring network (see `../05_MENTORSHIP/`). Taylor & Francis lists
AuthorAID among its development initiatives for exactly this reason. If you are eligible, this
is the single highest-value entry in the whole courses folder.
