| **Format** | Self-paced online courses on peer review and research integrity |

## What it covers

Peer-review training for early-career researchers — how to review manuscripts including
literature reviews — plus research-integrity modules. It is the successor to the **Publons
Academy**, which Clarivate retired (announced in 2024) and folded into this hub.

## Why it is recommended here

The historical home of reviewer training: Publons trained a generation of reviewers and
certified their reviews on the platform that became Web of Science researcher profiles. The
Academy continues that mission with free courses — and your completed training links to your
researcher record, which editors increasingly check when inviting reviewers.

## How to use it with ARES

- Take the peer-review course before running `skills/06_quality_fortress/adversarial_peer_review.md`
- Your certified reviewer profile supports the community-half of a publishing career — see `skills/10_impact_amplifier/`
