## What it covers

Per the OCW page: the basics of working with sources — summarizing and paraphrasing, synthesizing
source materials, citing, quoting, and avoiding plagiarism — plus technical-writing craft for
graduate students. The full assignment sequence is downloadable.

## Why it is recommended here

The only entry in this catalog where a top university's *entire graduate writing course* —
readings, notes, and assignments — is free with no platform account. It is the reference
implementation of "source-work" (summarize/paraphrase/synthesize/cite), which is both the core
of literature-based writing and the place most integrity failures happen.
