# Springer Nature Author Tutorials

| | |
|:---|:---|
| **Provider** | Springer Nature |
| **Platform** | <https://www.springernature.com/gp/authors/tutorials> |
| **Cost** | **Free** |
| **Format** | Concise, interactive tutorials ("concise introductions to the publishing process"), plus free webinars and a 5-module book-publishing mini-course |

## What it covers

How to write, submit, and publish a scholarly or scientific paper — the interactive *How to
write a research article* tutorial being the flagship (re-launched April 2020 as "new and
improved"). Complementary mini-courses and webinars cover structuring an article, choosing a
journal, and understanding editorial processes; a separate free 5-module course covers book
publishing.

## Why it is recommended here

A publisher's own free on-ramp, lighter than a MOOC but written by the people who run
*Scientific Reports*, *BMC*, and the Springer journal portfolios. Ideal as the "one evening"
orientation before committing to an 8-week course, and the natural companion to ARES's
`templates/Springer.md`.

## How to use it with ARES

- Complete the research-article tutorial, then draft against `templates/Springer.md`
- The journal-choice tutorial → `skills/07_journal_strategy/venue_matcher.md`
