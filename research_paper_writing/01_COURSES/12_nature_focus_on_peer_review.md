## What it covers

The role of a peer reviewer, the foundations of a good review, and how the review process
supports science — Nature's own training for the reviewer's side of the desk. Nature's on-demand
catalogue lists it as the free entry point to their peer-review training line.

## Why it is recommended here

This is *the* journal-editor-perspective course, from the publisher of *Nature* itself, at zero
cost. Where Stanford Unit 6 gives you one unit on peer review, this is a dedicated course from
the editors who run the process at a flagship journal family. Harvard's career-navigator listing
notes it is free with institutional access arrangements as well.

## How to use it with ARES

- Take this *before* running `skills/06_quality_fortress/expert_paper_reviewer.md` — you will understand what the simulated board is doing
