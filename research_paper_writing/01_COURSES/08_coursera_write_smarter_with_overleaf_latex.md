## What it covers

Efficient document creation with Overleaf and LaTeX — the preparation layer for any journal that
expects LaTeX (IEEE, ACM, Springer LNCS, arXiv, and the NeurIPS family). Module 1 covers
"Writing with Overleaf and Elements of LaTeX" per the course page.

## Why it is recommended here

ARES ships seven venue templates, and the LaTeX-based venues assume fluent Overleaf use. This is
the only dedicated, currently-listed Overleaf+LaTeX MOOC on a major platform — a practical gap-filler
between "I can write" and "I can submit."

## How to use it with ARES

- Learn Overleaf here, then apply `templates/IEEE.md`, `templates/ACM.md`, `templates/Springer.md`, `templates/arXiv.md`
