## What it covers

Step-by-step guides to writing a research paper, structured as learning paths; the catalog spans
manuscript preparation, ethical issues, submission, and peer response. Wiley Researcher Academy
courses are also listed on the platform (per its instructor page), making it a multi-publisher hub.

## Why it is recommended here

The most generous free tier among the commercial academies (20 full courses), with the option to
scale into 100+ courses and integrated AI tools under one subscription. The learning-path
organization (beginner → advanced → expert) matches how this library's own `08_LEARNING_PATHS/`
is built, so you can slot the free courses into a staged plan.

- Its citation-accuracy course ("How To Provide Correct Citations…") pairs with `skills/09_citation_mastery/multi_format_citations.md`
