## What it covers

An introduction to writing about science — nature, medicine, and technology — for general
readers: exploring how lay audiences encounter science, and building a foundation for strong
public-facing science writing.

## Why it is recommended here

Modern publishing rewards scientists who can also communicate beyond their subfield — press
releases, plain-language summaries, social threads, outreach. This is MIT's dedicated course for
that skill, free. It is the "Impact Amplifier" half of a writing education: Stanford Unit 8
covers the interview side; this course covers the writing side.
