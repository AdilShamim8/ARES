## What it covers (6 modules)

1. **Getting Started** — choosing and refining a research topic
2. **Research and Annotated Bibliography** — gathering and annotating sources
3. **First Draft and Introduction Paragraph**
4. **The Rough Draft**
5. **Revise and Edit** (per the course page's module list: "Revise and …")
6. Final polish toward a complete research paper

## Why it is recommended here

Listed separately because you can take *just this course* out of the specialization when you
already write serviceable English but have never carried a project from topic choice to finished
paper. The milestone structure (bibliography → first draft → rough draft → revision) forces the
staged production rhythm that experienced authors actually use, which is exactly what ARES's
workflows encode over longer timelines.
