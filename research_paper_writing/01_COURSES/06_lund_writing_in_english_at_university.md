# Writing in English at University — Lund University

| | |
|:---|:---|
| **Provider** | Lund University (Faculty of Engineering, LTH) |
| **Platform** | Coursera — <https://www.coursera.org/learn/writing-english-university> |
| **Cost** | Free to audit; paid certificate optional |
| **Format** | Self-paced MOOC on the conventions of academic English |

## What it covers

Per the course page: understanding the conventions of academic writing in English — its
components and benefits — to prepare for future writing tasks at university and in professional
life. Lund's own pages describe it as a resource for students preparing for academic writing
across disciplines, and the university lists it among its open online courses (last updated
March 2024).

## Why it is recommended here

A compact, single-course (rather than 5-course) route to academic-English conventions from a
leading European university. For international researchers who want European framing and
shorter commitment than the UC Irvine specialization, this is the standard pick; it appears on
Lund's own open-courses page and in MOOC directories.
