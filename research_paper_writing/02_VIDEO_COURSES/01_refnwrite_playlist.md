## Why this is the library's anchor

The user of this library chose this playlist as its seed — and it earns the role: it is a
complete, section-by-section walkthrough in under an hour, from a channel run by the makers of
the Ref-n-Write academic-writing software (whose paraphrasing tool and phrasebank this library
also profiles in `../06_TOOLS/`). One video per manuscript section means you can watch exactly
the video for the section you are writing tonight. Study notes for each video live in
[`video_notes/`](video_notes/).

## How to use it with ARES

Watch the video for your current section → open the matching study note → run the matching
`skills/05_writing_excellence/` prompt → score with `evaluators/CLARITY_ANALYZER.md`. The video
teaches, the note summarizes, ARES enforces.
