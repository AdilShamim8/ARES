| | |
|:---|:---|
| **Channel** | <https://www.youtube.com/@TheRedHeadedAcademic> |
| **Host** | Kaelyn Apple — History and African American Studies PhD student at Yale |
| **Cost** | **Free** |

## What it covers

Thesis logistics, graduate-level resumes/CVs, statements of purpose, and writing samples — the
application-and-milestone documents that surround research writing. Baylor's GWC recommends her
as "a gateway rather than a gatekeeper to higher education."

## Why it is recommended here

Strong writing samples win funding and admission, and thesis logistics (committee management,
milestone documents) are writing projects too. For researchers at those transitions, this
channel covers what pure paper-writing channels skip.
