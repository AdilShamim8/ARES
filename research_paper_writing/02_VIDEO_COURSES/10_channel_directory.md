## How to combine them

1. **Anchor**: Ref-n-Write playlist — one video per section, watch as you draft.
2. **Depth**: Karen McKee or ACS Author University for the section you're stuck on.
3. **Meta-game**: Andy Stapleton weekly, for journal strategy and reviewer psychology.
4. **Sustain**: Dr Amina Yonis + PhD and Productivity for the daily practice layer.
5. **Level the field**: Brisa Marie + TheRedHeadedAcademic for the surrounding academic system.

Plus one study-note file per Ref-n-Write video: [`video_notes/`](video_notes/).
