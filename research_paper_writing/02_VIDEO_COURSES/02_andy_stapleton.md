## What it covers

Candid, practitioner-level advice on succeeding in doctoral programs and early-career academia:
grant applications, professional portfolios, peer review, journal choice, publishing strategy,
and — more recently — how AI is changing academic research and writing. Baylor's Graduate
Writing Center lists it among its six recommended academic-writing channels, praising "candid
advice" from a former tenured professor.

## Why it is recommended here

The best signal-to-noise on *academic career mechanics* around publishing — the meta-game
no course teaches: which journal actually fits, how editors think, what reviewers reward, when
to push back. Pair the MOOCs (how to write) with this channel (how the game is played).
