# Dr Amina Yonis — YouTube channel

| | |
|:---|:---|
| **Channel** | <https://www.youtube.com/c/DrAminaYonis> |
| **Host** | Dr. Amina Yonis — PhD in Cancer and Cell Biology (UCL); founder of **The Page Doctor** (academic support company, ≈2.97K subscribers on its own channel) |
| **Cost** | **Free** videos; paid services via The Page Doctor |
| **Format** | Weekly-ish videos on writing productivity, tackling major writing tasks, balancing PhD priorities |

## What it covers

How to actually *get writing done* inside a PhD: breaking major writing tasks into sessions,
productivity systems, balancing priorities — plus deep-dive guides on paper structure. Baylor's
GWC listing notes the channel started during her own doctorate and covers "tackling major
writing tasks, maximizing productivity, and balancing priorities."

## Why it is recommended here

The best channel on the *psychology* of paper writing: starting, sustaining momentum, and
finishing. Courses teach structure; this channel teaches the daily practice that gets a
manuscript from 0% to submitted. Her company also offers the mentorship escalation path
(`../05_MENTORSHIP/03_the_page_doctor.md`).

## How to use it with ARES

- Adopt one productivity system from her videos, then run ARES workflows (which are already time-boxed) inside it
- Her paper-structure videos pair with `skills/05_writing_excellence/SKILL.md`
