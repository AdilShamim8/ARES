## What it covers

Tools and tutorials for organizing writing and research: keyword searching, source management,
note-taking systems, reading strategies, and synthesizing literature — the *workflow* layer
before drafting. Baylor's GWC recommends it for "tools and tutorials for organizing writing and
research."

## Why it is recommended here

A paper is only as good as the literature system behind it. This channel is the practical guide
to building that system (Zotero, note-taking, synthesis boards) — which then feeds every
`skills/02_literature_command/` prompt in ARES.

## How to use it with ARES

- Build your literature workflow from these videos, then run `skills/02_literature_command/citation_network_analysis.md` over the collected sources
