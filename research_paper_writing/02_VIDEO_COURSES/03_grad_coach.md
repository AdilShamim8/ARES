## What it covers

Plain-language, strategy-focused videos on research questions, academic voice, writing styles,
research proposals, and methodology chapters — the dissertation/thesis layer that sits just
beneath journal articles. Baylor's Graduate Writing Center recommendation notes the channel
covers "all things dissertation, thesis and research-related" with "actionable advice."

## Why it is recommended here

The clearest methodology explainer on YouTube: their methodology-chapter video is the single
most-watched treatment of that topic. If your paper's Methods or study-design section is weak,
this channel is the fastest fix — and their coaching arm (see `../05_MENTORSHIP/`) is the
escalation path.
