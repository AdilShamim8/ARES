## What it covers

Expert guidance across the whole writing craft: memorable papers, distilling findings into a
clear message, abstracts, introductions, and writing mechanics — framed by decades as author,
reviewer, and editor (her channel's own description).

## Why it is recommended here

The deepest individual expert channel on scientific writing specifically (not general academia):
one veteran author's systematic treatment, with playlists organized by writing task that mirror
this library's section guides.
