# ACS Author University — video series (ACS Publications)

| | |
|:---|:---|
| **Producer** | American Chemical Society Publications |
| **Access** | **Free** — via ACS Author University hub and the ACSPublications YouTube/Facebook video library |
| **Format** | Short episodes from ACS editors: finding your research story, starting a paper, figures and graphics, authorship order, open access (ACS AuthorChoice), handling rejection |

## What it covers

Practicing ACS journal editors (including editors of flagship journals) dispense episode-length
advice on each decision point of a chemistry manuscript: *How Do You Start Writing a Paper?*,
*Deciding Authorship Order*, figures/graphics preparation, open-access options, and what to do
with rejections. UWM's technical-writing guide lists ACS Author University as a core resource
for "videos and articles from the editors on tips for scientific writing, handling rejections,
funding your research."

## Why it is recommended here

Editor-voiced, chemistry-specific writing guidance — the discipline counterpart to the general
channels. The "find the story" episode is the single best short treatment of narrative logic in
a chemistry paper.
