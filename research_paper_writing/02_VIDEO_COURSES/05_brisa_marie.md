| | |
|:---|:---|
| **Channel** | <https://www.youtube.com/@BrisaMarie> (Future Ph.D.) |
| **Host** | Brisa Marie — first-generation PhD candidate in Culture and Performance at UCLA |
| **Cost** | **Free** |

## What it covers

Demystifying the hidden curriculum of doctoral study — the gatekeeping, unspoken expectations,
and unwritten norms that first-generation and under-represented researchers face: how academia
actually works, how to ask for help, how to survive the writing load. Recommended by Baylor's
Graduate Writing Center for exactly this role.

## Why it is recommended here

Writing fails most often for *social* reasons — nobody told you how the game works. For
first-generation researchers this channel is the mentor that levels the field, and its
emotional-realism about the writing process complements the technique-first courses.
