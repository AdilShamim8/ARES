| | |
|:---|:---|
| **Developer** | Ref-n-Write (<https://www.ref-n-write.com>) |
| **Cost** | Freemium (paid product with free training resources; see site for current pricing) |

| **Free layer** | The blog (<https://www.ref-n-write.com/blog>), training videos (<https://www.ref-n-write.com/trainingvideos>), and the YouTube channel that hosts this library's anchor playlist |

## What it covers

The company describes it as an award-winning tool for writing research papers and theses:
**cross-referencing** past papers while you write, an **academic phrasebank**, an
**AI-assisted paraphrasing tool** "specifically designed for academic writing," and
**plagiarism checking**.

## Why it is recommended here

It is the only tool built around the *phrasebank + paraphrase* workflow (the same workflow the
Manchester Phrasebank and this library's section guides teach), and its channel produced the
anchor playlist. The cross-referencing feature — pulling up your own source PDFs next to your
draft — attacks the exact failure mode of writing "from memory" (which produces accidental
plagiarism and vague citations).

## How to use it with ARES

- Cross-reference sources while drafting sections; then `skills/09_citation_mastery/bibliography_builder.md` formalizes the citations
- Paraphrase-check against `skills/08_ethics_compliance/` standards before submission
