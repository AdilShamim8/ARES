## What it covers

Per the site: an AI research assistant that runs **systematic literature reviews over 280M+
papers** and supports writing "papers with cited sources" — literature search, paper
comprehension (explanations of academic text in simple words), extraction of findings, and an
AI writing layer grounded in those sources.

## Why it is recommended here

The strongest *literature-grounded* assistant: where generic LLMs hallucinate citations,
SciSpace's write-with-citations loop pulls from an indexed corpus — the same discipline ARES
enforces manually with its evidence floor. Its explain-this-paper function is also the best
comprehension aid while reading for a review.

## How to use it with ARES

- Use SciSpace for the collection/comprehension phase of `skills/02_literature_command/`; switch to ARES prompts for synthesis and drafting
- Never let any AI citation into your manuscript without resolving it — cross-check with `skills/09_citation_mastery/bibliography_builder.md`
