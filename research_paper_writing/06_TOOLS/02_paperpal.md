| | |
|:---|:---|
| **Developer** | Cactus Communications (Editage family) |
| **URL** | <https://paperpal.com> (researcher plans: <https://paperpal.com/paperpal-for-researchers>) |
| **Cost** | Freemium — free tier with usage limits; paid plans for heavier use (site lists current pricing) |
| **Platform** | Web app; Word add-in; submission-readiness checks |

## What it covers

Per the product pages: AI-powered drafting and refinement for researchers — academic-tone
grammar and clarity edits, paraphrasing, plagiarism checks, reference-error checks, AI-generated
text detection, and a suite that "confirm[s] journal submission readiness" with checks "built
for top journals."

## Why it is recommended here

The most journal-aware of the AI assistants: it's built by a company whose main business is
academic editing, so its checks target submission-time failure modes (reference mismatches,
journal compliance) rather than generic prose. Its AI-text-detection feature also matters in the
2026 era of disclosure policies.

## How to use it with ARES

- Use ARES for *structure and argument* (the pipeline), Paperpal for *surface polish* (the last pass) — don't invert the order
- Run its submission-readiness suite after `evaluators/` gates pass, before the venue-template compliance check
