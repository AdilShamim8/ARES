# Tool comparison matrix

| Tool | Layer | Cost | Does | Don't use it for |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| [Ref-n-Write](01_refnwrite.md) | Drafting | Freemium | Cross-reference sources next to your draft; phrasebank; academic paraphrase | Literature discovery |
| [Paperpal](02_paperpal.md) | Polish | Freemium | Submission-grade grammar/clarity, reference checks, AI-text detection | Structure decisions |
| [SciSpace](03_scispace.md) | Literature | Freemium | Search & comprehension over 280M+ papers; write-with-citations | Final polish |
| [Overleaf](04_overleaf.md) | Production | Free tier | Collaborative LaTeX with venue templates | Reference management |
| [Zotero](05_zotero.md) | Library | Free/OSS | Collect → organize → cite → share; BibTeX export | Drafting prose |
| ARES (parent repo) | Pipeline | Free (MIT) | 10 skills, 4 evaluators, 7 templates, 4 workflows — discipline end-to-end | Surface grammar fixes |

## The recommended stack (free-first)

1. **Discover & comprehend** — SciSpace
2. **Collect & manage** — Zotero
3. **Draft with discipline** — ARES skills (+ Ref-n-Write if you want the phrasebank in-Word)
4. **Produce** — Overleaf with the venue template
5. **Polish** — Paperpal's free tier
6. **Gate-check** — ARES evaluators (Novelty ≥70, Rigor ≥75, Clarity ≥80, Impact ≥75)

Total cost of the recommended stack: **$0**. Every paid upgrade is optional throughput, not
capability.

*All verified 5 November 2026.*
