# 00 · Getting Started — the 12-step roadmap

**This folder is the guided program.** Twelve steps, three phases, eight weeks — each step
teaches one idea, assigns real work, and ends with an exit test. Start with
[`00_START_HERE.md`](00_START_HERE.md), then work the steps in order.

## Phase 1 · Foundations (Week 1)

| Step | Title | Time | Deliverable |
|:---:|:---|:---:|:---|
| 1 | [Set up your toolkit](step_01_set_up_your_toolkit.md) | 1 h | Zotero + Phrasebank live, 3 papers saved |
| 2 | [Know the paper's anatomy](step_02_know_the_paper_anatomy.md) | 45 min | A real paper dissected: 9 parts labeled, gap sentence found |
| 3 | [Read papers like a researcher](step_03_read_papers_like_a_researcher.md) | 90 min | Five reading notes (your first evidence pile) |
| 4 | [Find your research question](step_04_find_your_research_question.md) | 90 min | One RQ passing four tests + a three-sentence gap statement |

## Phase 2 · Write the paper (Weeks 2–5)

| Step | Title | Time | Deliverable |
|:---:|:---|:---:|:---|
| 5 | [Write the literature review](step_05_write_the_literature_review.md) | 2–3 h | Synthesis matrix for 8–10 papers + two synthesis paragraphs |
| 6 | [Write the methods](step_06_write_the_methods.md) | 2 h | Methods draft passing the five-category audit |
| 7 | [Write the results](step_07_write_the_results.md) | 2–3 h | Three finding paragraphs + one audited figure |
| 8 | [Write Introduction & Discussion](step_08_write_introduction_and_discussion.md) | 3 h | Funnel intro + six-move discussion, overclaims struck |
| 9 | [Write conclusion, abstract, title](step_09_write_conclusion_abstract_title.md) | 2 h | Three-sentence conclusion + five-move abstract + checked title |

## Phase 3 · Finish and submit (Weeks 6–8)

| Step | Title | Time | Deliverable |
|:---:|:---|:---:|:---|
| 10 | [Edit and self-review](step_10_edit_and_self_review.md) | 3 h | Draft ≥10% shorter, read-aloud clean, evaluator gates passed |
| 11 | [Choose the journal & cover letter](step_11_choose_journal_and_cover_letter.md) | 2 h | Three-venue worksheet + four-paragraph letter |
| 12 | [Submit and survive peer review](step_12_submit_and_survive_peer_review.md) | 2 h | Complete packet + rehearsed reviewer response + waiting plan |
