# START HERE — you are 12 steps from your first submitted paper

**Welcome.** This library takes a complete beginner — no publications, maybe no research
experience — to a polished, submitted manuscript in **8 weeks part-time**, one step at a
time, with real work at every step.

## The three promises

1. **Never lost.** You always know exactly which step you are on and what "done" looks like —
   every step ends with an exit test.
2. **Real work, not just reading.** Each step produces an artifact: notes, a matrix, a
   paragraph, a checklist. Watching videos is not the work; the exercises are.
3. **Real sources only.** Every course, video, book, and tool this roadmap points to was
   verified against the live web (see
   [`../09_SOURCE_VERIFICATION/`](../09_SOURCE_VERIFICATION/)). Nothing is invented.

## How a step file works

Every step file (in this folder) has the same anatomy, so you never have to re-learn the
format:

| Part | What it gives you |
|:---|:---|
| Header table | Phase, time cost, deliverable, prerequisite |
| **The one idea** | The single principle the whole step teaches |
| Why this step matters | The failure mode it prevents — in reviewer's terms |
| What you will do | Numbered actions |
| **Your work** | The exercises: templates to fill, artifacts to produce |
| Practice example | A hypothetical study showing the artifact done right |
| **Exit test** | Checkboxes that must all pass before the next step |
| Going deeper | Library links *only if* you want more than the step |

## Your companion manual — THE WRITING ROADMAP

While the steps above give you the *schedule*, [`../THE_WRITING_ROADMAP.md`](../THE_WRITING_ROADMAP.md)
is the *manual*: how to actually write each part of the paper — the reading-note template,
the RQ ladder, the academic writing style (clutter, verbs, sentences — the Stanford craft),
and one full section-by-section treatment (abstract, introduction, methods, results,
discussion, and the rest) with each rule traced to the course, video, or book it comes
from. Open it whenever a step says "write" and you want the how.
