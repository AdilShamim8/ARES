# Overview — what this library is

## The one-paragraph version

This library is the **learning half of ARES** — and it is built around a **12-step roadmap**
([`00_START_HERE.md`](00_START_HERE.md)) that takes a beginner to a submitted paper in 8
weeks of part-time work. Everything else in these 124 files is reference material that the
steps call on when needed. The parent repository gives you an AI operating
system that drafts, scores, and stress-tests manuscripts; this library gives *you* the education
to drive it well. It is a curated catalog of the world's best research-paper-writing resources —
**25 structured courses, 19 video resources, 8 books, 8 free guides, 5 mentorship programs, and
6 software tools** — each profiled in its own file with verified facts and a source log.

## Design principles

1. **Real-world data only.** Every course rating, module count, price, and subscriber figure in
   this library was read from the live web on **5 November 2026** and is quoted with its source
   URL. No fact here relies on memory or guesswork. If a platform does not publish a number, the
   profile says "not published" instead of estimating.
2. **Free-first.** Where a free option exists (audit tracks, free platforms like the Manchester
   Phrasebank, open courseware like MIT OCW), it is listed and the paid tier is clearly marked.
3. **Mentor-shaped.** The user of this library asked for "mentor with course and website
   information worldwide" — so every profile explains *who* teaches, *what* they cover, and
   *where* to ask a human for feedback (see `05_MENTORSHIP/`).
4. **Roadmap-first.** The 12 steps in this folder are the path; the typed shelves
   (course / video / book / guide / mentor / tool) are called on *by the steps*, so you never
   browse cold or wonder what to do next.
5. **Paired with ARES.** Each profile ends with a "how to use it with ARES" note that maps the
   resource onto the ten ARES skills, so learning and producing stay synchronized.

## The three tiers of resources

| Tier | What it means | Examples |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Tier 1 — Foundations (free)** | Everything you need to write a first publishable paper, at zero cost | Stanford *Writing in the Sciences* (audit), Wiley 14-module guide, Ref-n-Write playlist, Purdue OWL, Manchester Phrasebank, MIT OCW |
| **Tier 2 — Professional depth** | Publisher-grade training and reference books, some paid | Nature Masterclasses (institutional/free-sample), Elsevier & Wiley academies (free), Gastel & Day / Schimel / Wallwork books |
| **Tier 3 — Human mentorship** | A person reads your work and answers your questions | AuthorAID mentoring (free, LMIC-focused), Grad Coach, The Page Doctor, Nature workshops |

Most researchers should complete Tier 1 before buying anything in Tier 2 or 3.
