# Step 1 of 12 — Set up your toolkit

| | |
|:---|:---|
| **Phase** | 1 · Foundations (Week 1) |
| **Time** | ≈ 1 hour, once |
| **Deliverable** | A working reference library with 3 saved papers + a phrase bank you can reach in one click |
| **Before you start** | Nothing. This is the absolute first step. |

> **The one idea of this step:** professionals never retype references and never write "from
> memory." Two free tools remove those two failure modes before you write a single sentence.

## Why this step matters

Beginner papers fail in two predictable places long before a reviewer judges the science:

1. **Citation chaos** — references retyped by hand, formats mixed, a source lost halfway
   through revision. Fix: a reference manager (**Zotero**, free and open source).
2. **Vague academic English** — knowing *what* you want to say but not *how* academic writing
   says it, which leads to copying sentences from papers (a plagiarism risk). Fix: a phrase
   bank (**University of Manchester Academic Phrasebank**, free) that hands you the sanctioned
   phrasing for every rhetorical move.

Setting these up *first* means every later step produces clean artifacts automatically.

## What you will do

1. **Install Zotero** (≈15 min) — download from <https://www.zotero.org> for your platform,
   plus the **browser connector** (Zotero offers it for Chrome/Firefox/Edge/Safari on the same
   download page). Create a free account so your library syncs.
2. **Create a collection** named after your topic or question, e.g. `thesis-2026`.
3. **Save three real papers** into it (use the connector's button while viewing any paper
   page — PubMed, arXiv, IEEE, ScienceDirect all work).
4. **Bookmark the Phrasebank** — <https://www.phrasebank.manchester.ac.uk> — and open its
   *Introducing work* section once so you have seen the layout.
5. **(Optional but recommended) Install ARES** — open [`../../MASTER_PROMPT.md`](../../MASTER_PROMPT.md)
   from the repository root and paste it into your AI assistant (Claude or any capable LLM),
   following [`../../SETUP.md`](../../SETUP.md). From Step 5 onward, each step names the ARES
   command to run alongside your manual work.

## Your work

### Work 1.1 — Build the first shelf (15 min)

Save any three papers from your field into your new Zotero collection. For each, open the item
in Zotero and confirm the metadata arrived complete (authors, year, journal, DOI). If a field
is empty, click it and fix it now — 30 seconds today saves an hour at submission.

**Check yourself:** `My collection shows 3 items, each with a DOI.`

### Work 1.2 — Phrasebank scavenger hunt (10 min)

Using <https://www.phrasebank.manchester.ac.uk>, find and write down one phrase you could
genuinely use for each of these three jobs:

```text
1. Introducing the importance of a topic:    e.g. "In recent years, … has received increased attention"
2. Describing what a study did:              e.g. "The experiment was conducted in order to …"
3. Reporting a finding:                      e.g. "The results showed a significant difference in …"
```

You now know how the bank is organized — by the *job* your sentence performs.

### Work 1.3 — Make the tools one keystroke away (5 min)

Pin the Zotero desktop app and the Phrasebank bookmark to your browser's bookmarks bar.
Friction kills tool use; this minute is the highest-leverage minute of the week.

## Exit test — do not continue until all are true

- [ ] Zotero installed, synced, with a collection containing ≥3 complete items
- [ ] Phrasebank bookmarked and opened at least once
- [ ] (If using AI) the ARES master prompt is pasted and the assistant responds to `STATUS`
