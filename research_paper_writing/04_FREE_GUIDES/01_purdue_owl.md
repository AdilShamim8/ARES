## What it covers

Per the OWL pages: detailed information on how to write research papers — discussing research
papers as a genre, choosing topics, and finding sources — plus the surrounding handout system:
academic writing conventions, wordiness exercises, modes of discourse, and citation styles
(MLA, APA, Chicago…).

## Why it is recommended here

The most-linked free writing reference on the web, maintained by a university writing lab. For
the *undergraduate* first paper it is the best free starting point; for graduate researchers its
value is the citation-style and genre handouts you revisit at polishing time.
