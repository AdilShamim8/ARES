## What it covers

How to publish with IEEE journals, conferences, magazines, and books: article structure for
engineering venues, authoring tools and templates (LaTeX/Word), reference formatting, and a
*Write in Technical English* tutorial with concrete rules ("write clear, simple sentences
without unnecessary words… define all acronyms at first use").

## Why it is recommended here

The publisher-side guide for the world's largest engineering publisher — the exact counterpart
to ARES's `templates/IEEE.md`. Its *Write in Technical English* page is the best compact
statement of technical-English style rules on any publisher's site.

## How to use it with ARES

- Draft against `templates/IEEE.md`, verify compliance with the Author Center's own checklists
- The technical-English tutorial → same edit passes as `evaluators/CLARITY_ANALYZER.md`
