# Harvard Writing Project — Resources for Students

| | |
|:---|:---|
| **Provider** | Harvard College Writing Program (Writing Project) |
| **URL** | <https://writingproject.fas.harvard.edu/pages/resources-students> |
| **Cost** | **Free** |
| **Format** | Discipline-specific writing guides; occasional workshops on argumentative writing including science writing and research papers |

## What it covers

Harvard's undergraduate-facing writing guides, organized by discipline — how each field's
papers argue, use evidence, and cite — plus Writing Center workshop offerings on topics such as
science writing and research papers.

## Why it is recommended here

The discipline-lens: most guides teach "the research paper"; Harvard's teach *how the sciences,
the humanities, and social sciences each define argument differently* — the meta-skill behind
choosing the right register for your target venue.
