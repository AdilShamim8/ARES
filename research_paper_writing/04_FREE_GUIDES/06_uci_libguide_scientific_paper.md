## What it covers

A librarian-curated guide to scientific-paper writing: recommended books (it lists Glasman-Deal
and Day & Gastel among others), citation tools, databases, and how-to sources — the library-side
companion to a writing course.

## Why it is recommended here

A model of what your *own* university library probably offers: subject guides that bundle
writing books, citation managers, and literature databases with institutional access notes.
Reading this one shows you what to look for locally, and its book list independently confirms
this library's bookshelf choices.
