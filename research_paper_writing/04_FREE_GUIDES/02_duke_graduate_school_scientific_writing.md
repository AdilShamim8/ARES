# Duke Graduate School Scientific Writing Resource

| | |
|:---|:---|
| **Provider** | Duke University Graduate School |
| **URL** | <https://sites.duke.edu/scientificwriting> |
| **Cost** | **Free** |
| **Format** | Short lessons with worked examples; explicitly "not absolute rules" — writing judged by whether intent is communicated |

## What it covers

Lesson-style guidance on effective scientific prose — clarity, structure, word choice — built
around the principle stated on the site: "If the intent of the writer is communicated, the
writing was effective, regardless of rules kept or broken." Community threads (ResearchGate
"best free course?" discussions) repeatedly name it alongside Stanford's course as a top free
resource.

## Why it is recommended here

The best *short-form* free guide: each lesson is minutes long with real before/after examples.
Where the OWL is encyclopedic, Duke is curated — the right size to read while eating lunch and
apply that afternoon.

## How to use it with ARES

- One lesson per day; apply to the paragraph you're currently drafting; verify with `evaluators/CLARITY_ANALYZER.md`
