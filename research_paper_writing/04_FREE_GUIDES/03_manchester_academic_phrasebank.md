# Academic Phrasebank — University of Manchester

| | |
|:---|:---|
| **Provider** | The University of Manchester |
| **URL** | <https://www.phrasebank.manchester.ac.uk> |
| **Cost** | **Free** (web version) |
| **Format** | Phrase bank organized by rhetorical function: introducing work, describing methods, reporting results, discussing findings, writing conclusions, referring to sources |

## What it covers

Per its own homepage: examples of the phraseological "nuts and bolts" of academic writing,
organized by the rhetorical moves each part of a paper performs — e.g., its *Referring to
sources* section lists phrases for citation moves and includes notes on literature-review
framing. Created by Dr. John Morley (Institute for Education, 2015 edition cited by 28+ works).

## Why it is recommended here

The single highest-utility free URL in this entire library. When you know *what* you want to say
but not how academic English says it, the Phrasebank has six pages of sanctioned phrasings. It
is the bridge between understanding (courses) and production (drafting) — and unlike AI
paraphrasers, every phrase is human-curated and discipline-neutral.
