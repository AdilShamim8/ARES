## What it covers

Live, field-specific training where working Nature Portfolio editors give individual feedback —
most valuably on your actual abstract. The Masterclasses site describes field-specific writing
and publishing training for small groups "including one-to-one abstract review."

## Why it is recommended here

The only mentorship-tier resource where the mentor is a *journal editor at a flagship venue*.
One round of editor feedback on an abstract targeted at a Nature Portfolio journal is the
highest-leverage paid interaction in this library.
