| | |
|:---|:---|
| **Provider** | Editage (Cactus Communications) |
| **URL** | <https://www.editage.com/insights> |
| **Cost** | **Free** (registration) |
| **Format** | Article library, expert Q&A, and resources organized for authors and journals |

## What it covers

Editage's own description: "a wealth of free academic research and publishing resources and a
one-stop guide for authors and others involved in scholarly publishing" — how-to articles on
every publishing stage, expert answers to author questions, and interview/thought pieces on
publishing practice.

## Why it is recommended here

The largest free *articles + answers* layer from a commercial editing house: when you have a
narrow question ("how do I respond to a reviewer who misread my stats?" or "what counts as
self-plagiarism here?"), Insights usually has a considered answer — a mentor-shaped resource at
zero cost, staffed by the same professionals who sell editing.
