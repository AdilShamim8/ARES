## What it covers

Hands-on coaching through every stage of an academic research project — research questions,
design, methodology chapters, and writing — from a team that describes itself as "your own
doctoral-qualified coach to guide you through every stage."

## Why it is recommended here

The strongest commercial option for *methodology* rescue: if your study design or methods
chapter is the blocker (the most common rejection reason), their methodology-focused coaching
is the escalation path after free resources. The free channel covers ~80% of the same knowledge;
pay when you need accountability on your specific project.
