# Learning path 05 — Clinical & public health researchers

**Who this is for**: clinicians, epidemiologists, and public-health researchers whose first (or
next) paper reports a clinical study, survey, or field investigation. **Total: ≈5 weeks.**
**All free.**

## Week 1 — Evidence literacy (≈4 h)

- **Yale's *Understanding Medical Research*** — study designs, confounding, absolute vs.
  relative risk — [`../01_COURSES/05_yale_understanding_medical_research.md`](../01_COURSES/05_yale_understanding_medical_research.md)

## Weeks 2–3 — Writing the study (≈6 h)

- **CDC/TEPHINET Scientific Writing Course** — 8 interactive modules, IMRAD in order, built
  for field epidemiology — [`../01_COURSES/15_cdc_tephinet_scientific_writing.md`](../01_COURSES/15_cdc_tephinet_scientific_writing.md)
- Ref-n-Write videos #5–#7 (methods, results, discussion) with notes
- ARES: `skills/03_methodology_architect/statistical_rigor.md` on your analysis; ethics sweep
  with `skills/08_ethics_compliance/` (IRB numbers, consent, registration — clinical venues check)

## Week 4 — Reporting standards (≈4 h)

- Trial/reporting guidelines for your design (CONSORT, PRISMA, STROBE families are the venue
  requirement layer); ARES's biomedical exemplar shows the finished product —
  [`../../examples/biomedical.md`](../../examples/biomedical.md)
- Section guides 06–08 with clinical reading

## Week 5 — Submission + mentorship (≈4 h)

- **Imperial College's *Navigating Clinical Research*** if you are still scoping the research
  career itself — [`../01_COURSES/25_imperial_college_edX_courses.md`](../01_COURSES/25_imperial_college_edX_courses.md)
- AuthorAID mentoring (free, LMIC-focused) — [`../05_MENTORSHIP/01_authoraid_mentoring.md`](../05_MENTORSHIP/01_authoraid_mentoring.md)
- Cover letter (guide 12) + authorship with CRediT (guide 13) — clinical journals are strict on both

## Completion checklist

☐ Evidence-literacy course done ☐ 8 CDC modules done ☐ Reporting-standard checklist applied
☐ Ethics sweep clean ☐ Mentor engaged
