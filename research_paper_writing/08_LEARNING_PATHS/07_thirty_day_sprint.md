# Learning path 07 — The 30-day sprint (deadline rescue)

**Who this is for**: you have a submission deadline in roughly a month and a draft that is not
ready. This path compresses the library's essentials into daily blocks. **≈90 minutes/day,
all free.**

## Week 1 — Diagnose and triage (days 1–7)

- Day 1: Run all four ARES evaluators on your draft — the scores are your triage list
- Day 2: Ref-n-Write videos for your two weakest sections + their study notes
- Days 3–7: One section guide per day, weakest first; fix that section; re-score
  (`evaluators/CLARITY_ANALYZER.md` daily)

## Week 2 — Evidence and methods hardening (days 8–14)

- Days 8–9: `skills/03_methodology_architect/statistical_rigor.md` pass; fix what it flags
- Days 10–11: **MIT 21W.794** source-work materials; audit every citation for claim-match —
  your biggest rejection risk on a deadline is a sloppy .bib
- Days 12–14: **Duke lessons** (2/day) on the flagged sentences; **Wiley course** modules for
  your two weakest components

## Week 3 — Polish and package (days 15–21)

- Days 15–17: Figures pass — guide 11 checklist on every display item
- Days 18–19: Abstract (guide 02 + video #9) — rewrite from scratch, then score
- Days 20–21: Title/keywords (guide 01) + cover letter (guide 12)

## Week 4 — Final gates (days 22–30)

- Days 22–24: Adversarial review — `skills/06_quality_fortress/adversarial_peer_review.md`;
  fix what survives rebuttal
- Days 25–26: Ethics/compliance sweep (guide 13): IRB, data availability, COI, CRediT
- Days 27–28: Venue compliance — template limits, reference style, file formats
- Day 29: A human read-through (Editage Insights Q&A for anything unresolved —
  [`../05_MENTORSHIP/05_editage_insights.md`](../05_MENTORSHIP/05_editage_insights.md))
- Day 30: Submit. Then sleep, then start path 03 properly — the sprint is rescue, not education

## Completion checklist

☐ All four gates green ☐ Citation audit done ☐ Compliance sweep clean ☐ Submitted on day 30
