# Learning path 02 — Master's researcher: from coursework to submission-ready

**Who this is for**: a master's student (or research assistant) who has written course papers
and now needs to produce thesis-grade work or a first journal/conference submission.
**Total: ≈8 weeks part-time. Free core + one optional paid book.**

## Weeks 1–2 — Foundation course (≈8 h)

- **Stanford's *Writing in the Sciences*** units 1–4 (sentence craft) —
  [`../01_COURSES/01_stanford_writing_in_the_sciences.md`](../01_COURSES/01_stanford_writing_in_the_sciences.md)
- Parallel videos #1–#4 for structure anchoring

## Weeks 3–4 — Structure and the thesis manuscript (≈8 h)

- Stanford units 5 (manuscript) + **École Polytechnique project course** start (outline +
  journal shortlist) — [`../01_COURSES/02_ecole_polytechnique_how_to_write_and_publish.md`](../01_COURSES/02_ecole_polytechnique_how_to_write_and_publish.md)
- Section guides 03–09 applied to your thesis chapters
- **Grad Coach** methodology videos for the methods chapter —
  [`../02_VIDEO_COURSES/03_grad_coach.md`](../02_VIDEO_COURSES/03_grad_coach.md)

## Weeks 5–6 — Evidence, statistics, and integrity (≈6 h)

- **Yale's *Understanding Medical Research*** if health-adjacent —
  [`../01_COURSES/05_yale_understanding_medical_research.md`](../01_COURSES/05_yale_understanding_medical_research.md)
- **MIT 21W.794** source-work materials —
  [`../01_COURSES/09_mit_graduate_technical_writing_workshop.md`](../01_COURSES/09_mit_graduate_technical_writing_workshop.md)
- ARES: `skills/03_methodology_architect/statistical_rigor.md` on your analysis plan

## Weeks 7–8 — Submission literacy (≈6 h)

- Stanford unit 6 (publishing, ethics, predatory journals)
- **Wiley's 14-module course** as pre-submission checklist —
  [`../01_COURSES/14_wiley_complete_guide_research_article.md`](../01_COURSES/14_wiley_complete_guide_research_article.md)
- Cover letter + authorship guides (12–13); optional: buy **Gastel & Day 9th ed.** as the
  keep-forever reference — [`../03_BOOKS/01_gastel_day_how_to_write_and_publish.md`](../03_BOOKS/01_gastel_day_how_to_write_and_publish.md)

## Completion checklist

☐ Stanford course finished ☐ Project-course artifacts (outline, journal choice, checklist)
☐ Full ARES evaluator pass (all four gates) ☐ Ethics/compliance sweep done
