# Learning path 03 — PhD candidate / early-career researcher

**Who this is for**: a doctoral candidate or ECR producing papers repeatedly. This path builds
a *repeatable personal system* — craft, reviewer instincts, publishing strategy, and mentorship
— not just one manuscript. **Total: one semester at ≈4 h/week.**

## Month 1 — Craft mastery (≈12 h)

- **Stanford *Writing in the Sciences***, all 8 units, exercises done honestly —
  [`../01_COURSES/01_stanford_writing_in_the_sciences.md`](../01_COURSES/01_stanford_writing_in_the_sciences.md)
- Read **Schimel's *Writing Science*** alongside (story craft) —
  [`../03_BOOKS/02_schimel_writing_science.md`](../03_BOOKS/02_schimel_writing_science.md)
- Weekly: **Andy Stapleton** channel for the publishing meta-game —
  [`../02_VIDEO_COURSES/02_andy_stapleton.md`](../02_VIDEO_COURSES/02_andy_stapleton.md)

## Month 2 — Reviewer instincts (≈12 h)

- **Nature *Focus on Peer Review*** (free) →
  [`../01_COURSES/12_nature_focus_on_peer_review.md`](../01_COURSES/12_nature_focus_on_peer_review.md)
- **Elsevier Certified Peer Reviewer Course** (certificate) →
  [`../01_COURSES/11_elsevier_researcher_academy.md`](../01_COURSES/11_elsevier_researcher_academy.md)
- Practice: review one published paper in your field per week with
  `skills/06_quality_fortress/expert_paper_reviewer.md`

## Month 3 — Publishing strategy + mentorship (≈12 h)

- **Nature *Publishing a Research Paper*** if institutionally available →
  [`../01_COURSES/13_nature_publishing_a_research_paper.md`](../01_COURSES/13_nature_publishing_a_research_paper.md)
- Springer tutorials + a Wiley module per week
- **Dr Amina Yonis** channel for the writing-practice layer —
  [`../02_VIDEO_COURSES/04_dr_amina_yonis.md`](../02_VIDEO_COURSES/04_dr_amina_yonis.md)
- Secure a mentor: **AuthorAID** if LMIC-eligible (free) —
  [`../05_MENTORSHIP/01_authoraid_mentoring.md`](../05_MENTORSHIP/01_authoraid_mentoring.md);
  otherwise a Nature Masterclasses workshop or departmental mentor
- Run one full ARES **FULL_PAPER_WORKFLOW** on your current project end-to-end

## Completion checklist

☐ Stanford certificate ☐ Peer-review certificate ☐ Two published-paper reviews written
☐ Mentor relationship active ☐ One manuscript through all four evaluator gates ☐ Personal
revision checklist written in your own words
