# 08 · Learning paths — sequences by career stage

Pick one path and follow it top to bottom; each names specific courses, videos, and books with
time estimates, and ends with a completion checklist. Do not mix paths — they overlap
deliberately, and running two in parallel doubles the overlap without doubling the learning.

| # | Path | Weeks | Free? | For whom |
|:---|:---|:---:|:---:|:---|
| 01 | [Undergraduate: first paper](01_undergraduate_first_paper.md) | 6 | ✓ | Writing your very first research paper |
| 02 | [Master's researcher](02_masters_researcher.md) | 8 | core ✓ | Thesis-grade work or first submission |
| 03 | [PhD / ECR system-builder](03_phd_ecr.md) | semester | ✓ | Producing papers repeatedly, building a personal system |
| 04 | [Non-native English speakers](04_non_native_english.md) | 6 | ✓ | Writing in English as a second+ language |
| 05 | [Clinical & public health](05_clinical_public_health.md) | 5 | ✓ | Clinicians, epidemiologists, field studies |
| 06 | [Engineering & CS](06_engineering_cs.md) | 4 | ✓ | IEEE/ACM/Springer/ML-conference authors |
| 07 | [30-day sprint](07_thirty_day_sprint.md) | 4 | ✓ | Deadline rescue for a draft in progress |

## Which one am I?

- Never written a paper → **01**
- Written course papers, now a thesis or first article → **02**
- Must publish repeatedly, want reviewer instincts and a mentor → **03**
- English is the barrier, not the ideas → **04** (can run alongside any other path)
- Your study involves patients, populations, or field data → **05**
- Your venue is a proceedings, not a journal → **06**
- The deadline is inside a month → **07** first, then a real path afterwards
