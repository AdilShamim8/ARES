# Learning path 01 — Undergraduate: your first research paper

**Who this is for**: an undergraduate writing your first research paper (course thesis, senior
capstone, or a first attempt at a short article). No publishing experience assumed. **Total
time: ≈6 weeks part-time. All resources free.**

## Week 1 — Orient (≈4 h)

- Watch the **Ref-n-Write playlist** videos #1–#3 (intro, introduction, literature review) —
  [`../02_VIDEO_COURSES/01_refnwrite_playlist.md`](../02_VIDEO_COURSES/01_refnwrite_playlist.md)
- Skim **Purdue OWL** *Writing a Research Paper* — [`../04_FREE_GUIDES/01_purdue_owl.md`](../04_FREE_GUIDES/01_purdue_owl.md)
- Read the section guides for Introduction and Literature Review —
  [`../07_SECTION_GUIDES/`](../07_SECTION_GUIDES/)

## Week 2 — Enroll and build the base (≈4 h)

- Enroll in **UC Irvine's *Project: Writing a Research Paper*** (free audit) —
  [`../01_COURSES/04_uci_project_writing_a_research_paper.md`](../01_COURSES/04_uci_project_writing_a_research_paper.md)
- Install **Zotero** and build your first collection — [`../06_TOOLS/05_zotero.md`](../06_TOOLS/05_zotero.md)
- Bookmark the **Manchester Academic Phrasebank** — [`../04_FREE_GUIDES/03_manchester_academic_phrasebank.md`](../04_FREE_GUIDES/03_manchester_academic_phrasebank.md)

## Weeks 3–4 — Produce the milestones (≈8 h)

- Follow the UCI capstone modules: topic → annotated bibliography → first draft
- Videos #4–#5 (research questions, methods) paired with their study notes
- Draft with ARES: run `skills/01_ideation/hypothesis_forge.md`, then `05_writing_excellence` prompts

## Week 5 — Results through conclusion (≈6 h)

- Videos #6–#8 + section guides 07–09
- **Duke's Scientific Writing Resource** lessons for sentence-level polish —
  [`../04_FREE_GUIDES/02_duke_graduate_school_scientific_writing.md`](../04_FREE_GUIDES/02_duke_graduate_school_scientific_writing.md)

## Week 6 — Abstract, title, and submission mechanics (≈4 h)

- Video #9 + section guide 02; then guides 01, 10, 12
- Score with `evaluators/CLARITY_ANALYZER.md`; iterate to ≥80
- Submit per your course's or venue's instructions

## Completion checklist

☐ First paper drafted with all nine parts ☐ Zotero library established ☐ Clarity ≥80 ☐ One
course enrolled for the next level (Stanford — path 03) ☐ Phrasebank is muscle memory
